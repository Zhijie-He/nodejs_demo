DAY 06 / THURSDAY↗
NEED→IDEA→TEST

Robotics Innovation
Workshop

机器人创新工作坊

Six teams. Four guided hours. Explore inspection, logistics or service robotics through user needs, feasibility and business models.

  1. Frame a real-world problem
  2. Shape a solution and collaboration opportunity
  3. Share a one-page concept in three minutes

约六组共创，产出一页英文方案与三分钟展示。无需编程或硬件操作经验，下午早些结束。
